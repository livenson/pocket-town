Bots you can read at a glance

Peg-folk live their own day. What they’re doing, thinking and planning is always on show, in pictures, so a six-year-old can predict and steer them.

Doing now

One icon above every bot. Its ring fills as the task finishes, so the child can tell “almost done”.

Thinking about

Cloud bubbles hold a picture only: a wish, not an action. Drop that thing on them and the wish comes true.

Together

Friends turn to face each other and swap emote bubbles in turn, so a conversation is visible without words.

Visiting

A doorbell chime, a bell bubble and a visitor flag on the roof. On the map the house pin shows the guest’s face.
